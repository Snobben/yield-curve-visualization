US Treasury Yield Curve

Watch how the US Treasury yield curve evolved throughout 2022, showing the relationship between bond maturity and interest rates. An inverted curve (short-term rates higher than long-term) often signals recession concerns.

Understanding the Treasury Yield Curve

Normal Curve

Upward sloping - longer-term bonds have higher yields. Indicates healthy economic expectations.

Inverted Curve

Downward sloping - short-term yields exceed long-term. Historically precedes recessions.

Flat Curve

Minimal difference between short and long-term rates. Suggests economic uncertainty.

Brent Crude Oil Futures Curve

Explore the evolution of Brent crude oil futures prices across different delivery months from 2023-2025. The curve shape reveals market sentiment about future supply and demand.

Understanding Futures Curves

Contango (Upward)

Future prices exceed spot prices. Normal market structure reflecting storage costs and stable supply.

Backwardation (Downward)

Spot prices exceed future prices. Indicates supply tightness or immediate strong demand.

Market Signals

Sharp changes in curve shape can signal major shifts in supply, demand, or geopolitical factors.
